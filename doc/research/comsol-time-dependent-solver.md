# COMSOL time-dependent solver: what the documentation established

This records what the COMSOL documentation was read to establish for `atstep`, and what it did not. Sources: the 6.0, 6.2 and 6.4 Reference Manuals (6.2 quoted unless a row says otherwise), the 6.4 chapter HTML, and the Structural Mechanics and AC/DC module guides.

Last read 2026-10, with the local COMSOL 6.2.0.290 solver readback of 2026-10-23. Quotations are gone; what remains is one row per claim that shaped the implementation, with the claim's status against the code and against the recorded runs in [COMSOL alignment](../comsol-alignment.md).

## What this file does not establish

- **The automatic scale of a multi-dof tolerance.** `sigma_i` comes from COMSOL's analysis of the model and is printed per dependent variable; the library cannot derive it, so the caller passes the scale in `ErrorTest::scale` (settings.hpp:145–156). The probes at comsol-alignment.md:119–130 are the only multi-dof evidence, and none of them is automatic.
- **The weighting of a multi-field system.** The field average over dofs is measured (doc/evidence/field-norm.md:293–309) but not implemented, and the velocity's re-inclusion under consistent initialization is neither implemented nor measured; the recorded reference is one scalar equation, so the re-inclusion cannot be checked here (comsol-alignment.md:180–181).
- **Generalized-α.** Its step-size sequence, its high-frequency damping (`rho_inf`) and its predictor choices are not stated in the fetched manuals, and the library has no such path, so nothing here constrains one.
